THE BRIEFING

The job is yours

In 1910, seven men met in secret on Jekyll Island and designed a central bank. You are what they built. Keep inflation near 2%, keep the street calm, and survive 48 months.

●
The needle. Green is calm. Blue is recession, red is inflation. Peg either end and your term ends.
▲▼
The lever. One interest rate, moved in 0.25 steps. It works slowly, like everything in this building.
⚡
Items. Washington speak, single use: speeches, blame, emergency buttons. They reset the street, briefly.
⛰
The trap. Cheap money builds projects that only work at cheap rates. They flatter GDP first, then bust when you hike.
❤
Five lives. When the needle pegs, an emergency meeting saves you. Five per term. The sixth crisis is final.
