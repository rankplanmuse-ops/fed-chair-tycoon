BUILD RULES

  • Dock is fixed and never scrolls: portrait + name, inflation readout, needle gauge, rate triangles + rate %, item button. Scene lives above it.
  • Fed always centred on the hill crest and never covered by HUD, chips, coin or title text: portrait band 15-25% of stage height, plate band 47-60%.
  • Gas station left, supermarket right, both fully inside the frame on portrait (no cropped storefronts). Signs stay blank in the art; the game paints live prices on top.
  • GDP and CPI bars overlay the scene edges (left and right, 24-55% height). Nothing important in the art may sit under them.
  • Wide plate is 4:3; the centre 42% of its width is the 9:16 crop. Fed, both shops and the road live inside that band; only scenery may fall outside.
  • HUD debt ticker and month pill sit on top of the sky. Price chips (gas, eggs, jobs, lives) sit under the HUD, over the sky and hill top.
